Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

📦 Orders & delivery

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 2 to 5 working days. You'll get tracking updates by email.

How do I track my order?

Open your order history and click the tracking link on the order.

Is delivery free?

Yes, on orders over KES 5,000. Smaller orders pay a small delivery fee shown at checkout.

💳 Payments

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Payments go through a trusted gateway using tokens, so your card details never touch our servers.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards and mobile money. The full list is shown at checkout.

↩️ Returns & refunds

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 14 days if it's unused and in its original packaging.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the item.

👤 Your account

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the login page and we'll email you a reset link.

How do I update my details?

Sign in and open your profile to change your name, email or addresses.

Still need help?

Send us a message and we'll reply soon.
