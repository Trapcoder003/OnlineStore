Our 14-Day Return Policy

Changed your mind? No problem. You can return any unused item within 14 days of delivery, provided it is in its original packaging with all tags attached.

  • Contact our support team to initiate a return.
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
  • Return shipping costs are covered by the customer unless the item was defective or incorrect.
  • Certain items (like perishables or intimate apparel) cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
